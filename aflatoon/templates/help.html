How to use this

Everything you need to do, in the order to do it.
The one rule. You type each thing once, in the screen where it belongs. Your cash and bank balances update themselves. Never enter the same money again on the Cash page — doing that twice is exactly how books get mixed up.

Do this first, today

1

Set your opening position

Count the cash in your drawer, check your bank balance, and type both in. This is where the books start.

Open it
2

Add your loans and EMIs

Add every loan you have. As many as you like, whenever you like. When you mark one paid, the money leaves your bank balance automatically.

Add a loan
3

Add the clothes you already own

One row is one piece. Do 20–50 at a time, no rush. You cannot record a sale for a piece that is not in the app, so this happens as you sell.

Bulk add
4

Change your password

In Settings, set a password only you know. Then nobody else can open your numbers.

Settings
About last year. Nothing from before today can be brought in — that trading was never written down anywhere, so there is nothing to import. This app is here to make sure that never happens again, starting now.

Every day, about 2 minutes at closing time

  1. Record each sale — Sales Entry. Cash and bank go up by themselves.
  2. Record your expenses — Expenses. Don't skip the small ones: tea, transport, packing tape. These are what add up. Tick Paid? = No if you have not paid it yet, and the money will not leave your balance until you do.
  3. Mark any EMI you paid — EMI Tracker. Choose cash or bank. It rolls to next month by itself.

Every time you buy stock

  1. Add the lot — Purchase Batches. How many pieces, what it cost in total, and how much you paid right now.
  2. Add the pieces — Add Stock in Bulk. The cost per piece is worked out from the lot, so you do not type it.
  3. Pay the rest later? Use the Pay button on that lot. Each payment keeps its own date, so your balance is right on the day the money actually moved.

Once a week

Count your money

Count the notes in the drawer and check the bank balance. Type both into the Count your cash box on the Cash page.

If the app says ₹7,400 and you count ₹7,350, that ₹50 gap is a sale or expense you forgot, or an amount typed wrong. This is how you catch it.

If it matches, your books agree with your money. Nothing beats that.

Use the Cash page only for these

Anything that has its own screen is added to cash automatically. The Cash page is for the handful of movements that have no other home.

MovementWhat to pickWhy it is here
Money you put in from your own pocketOwner Investment It is not income. This keeps it from looking like a profit.
Money you take out for yourselfOwner Withdrawal Spending is not a shop expense. This keeps profit honest.
Moving money between cash and bankTransfer The same money, in a different place.
Money returned to a customerRefund The sale is marked returned; this is the money going back.
Scrap, or anything that is not a garment saleOther Income Money in, but not from selling stock.
Spending your saved restock moneyMajor Reserve Spend Keeps the reserve report honest.

Not on this list: sales, expenses, EMI payments and supplier payments. Those are entered in their own screens and appear here automatically. Do not add them again.

If something looks wrong

  • Data Quality lists the problems worth fixing first — missing costs, prices or categories.
  • Anything you record can be edited or deleted. If you edit or delete a sale, expense or loan, the cash balance corrects itself with it.
  • The Cash page shows every movement with a running balance. Scroll it to see exactly where your money went.
